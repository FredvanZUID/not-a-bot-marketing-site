About us

How it started

Why we built not-a-bot.ai

It started with a consulting firm in Ghent. Eight people, a shared inbox, and a problem that kept getting worse: every day, two hours were going to the same client questions. Not complex ones. Not new ones. The same twelve questions, asked by different people, answered again and again by whoever had the misfortune of being near their email.

They tried every major chatbot platform. The results were not encouraging. Some gave confident, completely wrong answers — what the industry politely calls "hallucinations." Others required a developer to set up anything beyond a basic FAQ tree. Most charged €500 a month or more for the features that actually mattered.

So they built their own. Not as a startup idea. Not as a side project with ambitions. Just as a tool to solve their own problem.

Three years later, 500+ businesses across Belgium, the Netherlands and France use not-a-bot.ai. The core promise has not changed: an AI agent that knows your business, admits when it doesn't know something, and can be set up by a non-technical person in under 30 minutes.

We are still small, deliberately. We like it that way.

Our mission

Our mission

"Give every business — not just enterprises — an AI agent that actually knows what it's talking about."

Your knowledge is the advantage

Your business knowledge is your competitive advantage — not a generic AI's training data. Your pricing, your process, your voice. That's what your agent should know.

Honest uncertainty beats confident guessing

AI that admits it doesn't know something is more valuable than AI that guesses confidently. Hallucinations aren't a minor bug — they're a trust-destroying feature.

28 minutes, not 28 days

Self-service setup should mean 28 minutes, not 28 days. If your non-technical team member can't go live without a developer, the platform has failed — not the user.

Our approach

How we're different

Three things we built from the ground up — not bolted on as features.

No hallucinations, by design

We built our architecture around document-grounded retrieval. Your agent can only answer from content you've given it. When it doesn't know, it escalates. This isn't a nice-to-have — it's the foundation.

European-first, GDPR-first

Our infrastructure is in the EU. Our team is Belgian. We understand how KMOs in Belgium, the Netherlands and France think about privacy, compliance, and vendor relationships. GDPR is not an afterthought.

Self-service is a real feature

Most "easy to set up" platforms still require a developer for anything beyond basic. We measure success in minutes, not days. Average time to first live response: 28 minutes.

Principles

How we work

Ship fast, fix fast

We'd rather learn from a real customer than theorize in a meeting. Speed of learning matters more than speed of shipping.

Honest about limitations

Our agent escalates when it doesn't know. So do we. If we can't solve your problem, we'll say so directly.

Small teams, real ownership

8 people, no bureaucracy. Everyone owns something real and can see the impact of their work directly.

Customers are not users

We build relationships, not usage metrics. We know our customers' names and what they're trying to accomplish.

European identity

Compliance-first is not a constraint, it's a design principle. We build for how European businesses actually operate.

Simple over clever

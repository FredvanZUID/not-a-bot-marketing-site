Honest Comparison

not-a-bot.ai vs Tidio, Chatbase & Intercom

Which AI agent platform is right for your business? An honest comparison — including where not-a-bot.ai is NOT the best choice.

The short version

Choose not-a-bot.ai if...

  • You're a KMO/SMB in Europe
  • You need a no-hallucination guarantee
  • You want self-service setup without developers
  • Your budget is €19–€249/agent/month
  • GDPR compliance is non-negotiable
  • You work in professional services, HR, or hospitality

Choose Tidio if...

  • You run an e-commerce store on Shopify/WooCommerce
  • You need Shopify-native live chat integration
  • You have a US-based customer base
  • Basic AI is enough and you want built-in live chat

Choose Intercom if...

  • You're enterprise with 200+ employees
  • You have a dedicated CX operations team
  • You need the most proven platform regardless of cost
  • Budget is not the primary constraint

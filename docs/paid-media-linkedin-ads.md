# LinkedIn Ads — not-a-bot.ai
## Paid Media Playbook

**Prepared by:** ZUID Performance Marketing
**Date:** 2026-05-09
**Platform:** LinkedIn Campaign Manager
**Objective:** Trial starts (free 5-day trial), targeting BE/NL/FR B2B decision-makers

---

## Strategic Framing

LinkedIn is the primary B2B channel for not-a-bot.ai. The platform enables precise job-title and company-size targeting that Meta and Google cannot match. The audience is professional, intent-aware, and skews toward decision-makers who control tool adoption budgets under €50/month — a threshold that falls comfortably within individual discretionary spend for most SMB operators.

**Attribution note:** LinkedIn conversions are systematically underreported due to cross-device behavior. Expect 30–40% view-through attribution gap. Validate via GA4 direct/organic channel lift during campaign periods and cross-reference trial signups by date against LinkedIn impression curves.

**Fogg B=MAP application:**
- Motivation: Loss aversion (hours wasted on repetitive communication), anticipatory enthusiasm (what 5 free hours/week looks like)
- Ability: €19/month, 28-minute setup, no developer — maximum friction removal in every ad
- Prompt: Problem-state headlines that trigger recognition at the exact moment of professional frustration

| Parameter | Value |

## Campaign Governance

**Weekly review checklist:**
1. CTR per variant — pause if below 0.35% after 3,000+ impressions
2. CPL tracking — flag if >€80 after week 2
3. Frequency monitoring — reduce budget if >5 impressions/member/30 days
4. A/B winner identification — week 3 review, pause weakest performer
5. Budget reallocation — shift spend toward best-performing campaign (A, B, or C) after week 2

**Month-end review:** Produce CPL by campaign, variant performance ranking, trial-to-MQL quality score, and recommendation for month 2 budget split.

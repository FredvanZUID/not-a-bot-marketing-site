## Strategic Framing

Meta Ads serves a different function in the not-a-bot.ai mix than Google or LinkedIn. Google captures existing demand. LinkedIn targets job titles with precision. Meta creates demand — reaching business owners and professionals in a personal-context mindset, before they've searched for a solution.

The product's price point (€19/month) and setup simplicity (28 minutes, no developer) make it unusually suited to impulse-adjacent conversion on Meta. The challenge is interrupting a feed without being dismissed, and turning a passive scroll into a trial signup within 48–72 hours.

**Fogg B=MAP applied to Meta:**
- Motivation: Loss aversion shown through storytelling (the before-state they recognise), not product features
- Ability: €19/month + no developer + 28 minutes = friction floor. Every ad reinforces this.
- Prompt: Strong visual hook in the first second of video; bold contrast on static; question-based headlines that trigger self-recognition

**Meta 2026 algorithm notes:**
- Reels placements now deliver better reach and cost efficiency than static feed for awareness
- Meta AI optimisation performs well when given sufficient conversion data — allow 50+ events before drawing conclusions on Advantage+ performance
- For a new pixel with no conversion history: run traffic or engagement objective first for 7–14 days to seed the pixel, then switch to conversion objective

## Top of Funnel — Awareness

**Campaign objective:** Awareness or Traffic (depending on pixel maturity)
**Campaign budget:** €25/day, split across 3 ad sets (approximately €8/day per variant for first 2 weeks, then concentrate on winner)
**Ad sets:** A/B/C test — one audience per ad set (Core only in phase 1; introduce LAL in week 3)
**Bidding:** Lowest cost (let Meta optimise delivery)
**Frequency target:** 2–4 impressions per user per week (monitor in Ads Manager)

### TOF Variant 1 — Storytelling Video

**Format:** Vertical video (Reels + Feed), 30–45 seconds
**Placement:** Instagram Reels (primary), Facebook Reels, Feed

**Video Script:**

Hook (0–3 seconds, full screen text + voice-over):
"You just answered this question for the 50th time this month."

Act 1 — Before (3–15 seconds):
Show a business owner (architect/consultant aesthetic — workspace, rolled-up plans or a laptop with briefs visible) checking their phone at 10pm. Email notification sound. They open the inbox: stacked emails, several with identical subject lines ("Quick question about your services", "Do you do residential?", "What's your pricing?"). Slight grimace. They start typing a reply — same answer, again.

Cut to: the next morning. Same person, same inbox. New stack. Same questions.

Voice-over: "You've answered this before. You'll answer it again tomorrow. And the day after."

Act 2 — Recognition (15–22 seconds):
On-screen text: "What if your business could answer for you?"

Quick montage: business owner uploads a document (service brochure, FAQ sheet). A simple interface. A chat window populates with responses — instantly, accurately, sourced directly from their document.

Voice-over: "not-a-bot.ai trains an AI agent on your documents. It answers the questions. You focus on the work."

Act 3 — After (22–35 seconds):
Same business owner — same phone — but now at dinner. Phone buzzes: "3 questions answered automatically by your agent." They smile, put the phone down, turn back to the table.

Voice-over: "Live in 28 minutes. €19/month. No developer needed."

Closing frame (35–45 seconds):
Logo + URL full screen. Text: "Start your free 5-day trial." CTA button in Meta UI: "Learn More" leading to trial page.

**Caption (primary text):**
Your business questions deserve better than your personal time. not-a-bot.ai — AI agents trained on YOUR documents. Live in 30 minutes. Free trial, no credit card.

**CTA button text:** See how it works

**Creative brief:**
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (Reels primary), also export 1:1 for Feed
- Tone: warm, human, recognisable — not tech-demo aesthetic
- Subtitles: burned-in, white with dark outline (85% of Reels watched muted)
- No stock footage — shoot or direct with authentic workspace settings
- Text overlays: max 3 lines, large sans-serif, high contrast
- Do not show the full product UI in the first 10 seconds — emotion first, product second

**Placement:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed (1:1 and 1.91:1)

**Primary text:**
Stop answering the same question twice.

**Headline:**
AI agent trained on your business. Live in 28 minutes.

**Description:**
€19/month. No credit card. GDPR compliant.

**CTA button:** Learn More

**Creative brief:**
- Background: crisp white or very light grey
- Typography: large, bold, dark — one message per visual
- Visual element: a stack of identical email notification icons (progressively fading, suggesting the same question repeated) transitioning to a single clean chat bubble with a tick mark (automated and handled)
- Logo bottom-right, URL visible
- Text overlay on image: keep below Meta's text coverage limit (<20% of image area)
- Test two cropping versions: 1080×1080 (square, Feed) and 1080×1920 (vertical, Stories/Reels fallback)
- This is the "ugly but effective" variant — lo-fi creative often outperforms polished imagery in direct-response Meta campaigns (2026 industry data). Test without heavy production.

**Format:** Single static image or 15-second video
**Placement:** Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed

**Primary text:**
You visited not-a-bot.ai. Your free trial is still available. 5 days, no credit card required. Your first agent could be live before Friday.

**Headline:** Your free trial hasn't started yet

**Description:** 28-minute setup. €19/month after trial. Cancel anytime.

**CTA button:** Start free trial → https://not-a-bot.ai/

**Creative brief:**
- If video: 15 seconds max. Screen recording style — show the actual setup flow in real-time, compressed. "From zero to live in 28 minutes. For real." Authentic, not polished. This variant benefits from lo-fi production.
- If static: countdown-style visual. Large text: "5 days free." Sub-text: "Your trial is waiting." Clock icon. No clutter.
- 1080×1080px primary; 1080×1920px secondary (Stories)

**Framework:** Fogg Prompt + Cialdini Commitment/Consistency. The person already showed interest (site visit = micro-commitment). This ad closes the loop with a low-stakes action.

### BOF Variant 3 — Objection Handling

**Format:** Single static image or short video (20 seconds)
**Placement:** Facebook Feed

**Primary text:**
Common concern: "Our questions are too complex for AI."

Reality: 70–80% of business questions are repetitive. Your agent handles those — instantly, accurately, from your own documents. Complex ones go to your team — with context summarised.

The best part? You find out which is which in 5 days. Free.

**Headline:** Your questions probably aren't as unique as you think.

**Description:** Start free. See what your agent handles automatically. No surprises.

**CTA button:** Start your free trial

**Creative brief:**
- Conversational, slightly cheeky tone. Not defensive.
- If static: "Myth vs. Reality" split layout. Left: "Our questions are too complex." Right: "70–80% are exactly the same as everyone else's." Small footnote: "Based on not-a-bot.ai customer data." Clean, professional.
- If video: talking-head style or text-animation explainer — problem/solution in 20 seconds. Lo-fi works well here.
- 1080×1080px (static) or 9:16 (video)

**Framework:** This ad is a direct-response to the #1 stated objection in SaaS trials. Addressing it in copy rather than at signup reduces drop-off and pre-qualifies the lead.

### BOF Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Action if missed |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (link click) | 2.0–4.5% (retargeting) | If <1.5%, creative refresh required |

## Budget Summary and Phasing

### Monthly Budget Plan

| Campaign | Daily Budget | 4-Week Total | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOF — Awareness (3 variants) | €25/day | €700 | Reach, video views, LP traffic |
| BOF — Retargeting (3 variants) | €10/day | €280 | Trial starts |
| **Total** | **€35/day** | **€980** | |

### Phase Recommendations

**Week 1–2 (Seeding phase):**
- Run all three TOF variants simultaneously at equal budget split (€8/day each)
- Objective: Traffic or Video Views (not Conversions — pixel needs data first)
- BOF at €10/day using Core audience retargeting only (no LAL yet)
- Monitor: reach, frequency, CPM, video completion rate (TOF1), CTR (TOF2, TOF3)

**Week 3 (Optimisation phase):**
- Pause weakest TOF variant (lowest CTR + highest CPM)
- Increase budget on top-performer (+€5/day)
- Switch TOF objective to Conversions if pixel has recorded 50+ landing page views
- Activate Lookalike audience (2%) as second ad set — test against Core audience

**Week 4 (Consolidation phase):**
- Concentrate 80% of TOF budget on best-performing variant
- Review BOF frequency — if >5/week, pause and refresh creative
- Pull 4-week data: CPL, trial start rate, trial-to-active conversion
- Decision: continue, scale (max 20% budget increase to avoid pacing reset), or pause for creative refresh

**Text overlay rules (Meta 2026):**
- Meta no longer enforces the 20% rule algorithmically, but heavy text still reduces CPM
- Keep headlines and primary text doing the work — image/video should carry emotion, text carries context
- Always burn subtitles into video files — do not rely on Meta's auto-captions for conversion campaigns

## Pixel and Tracking Setup

**Priority actions before launch:**

1. Install Meta Pixel on https://not-a-bot.ai/ via GTM or direct code
2. Configure custom events:
   - `ViewContent` — key landing pages (use cases, pricing, how it works)
   - `Lead` — trial signup form open/start
   - `CompleteRegistration` — trial signup confirmed
3. Set up Conversions API (server-side) alongside Pixel — critical for iOS 17+ attribution accuracy
4. Test all events in Events Manager before launching conversion campaigns
5. Create Custom Audiences immediately (website visitors, trial starters) — audiences build over time and are needed for BOF and LAL

**Attribution window recommendation:**
- Click-through: 7 days
- View-through: 1 day
- Rationale: The product has a short decision cycle (low price, low commitment) — 1-day view-through is appropriate. B2C settings, not B2B window.

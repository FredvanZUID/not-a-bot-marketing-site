## Strategic Framing

Google Ads captures active demand — searchers who are already looking for a solution. For not-a-bot.ai, this means intercepting three distinct intent signals: (1) people searching the brand directly, (2) people researching competitor tools, and (3) people describing the pain they want to solve in their search query.

The 28-minute setup time and €19/month price point are uniquely suited to paid search because they neutralise the two most common objections at the moment of decision: "Is this complicated?" and "Is this expensive?" Both objections should appear in ad copy wherever possible.

**Attribution note:** Start with Maximize Clicks while the account builds conversion data. Migrate to Target CPA after 30+ trial-start conversions have been recorded. Do not switch bidding strategies before this threshold — premature smart bidding causes CPA instability.

**Conversion tracking requirements (validate before launch):**
- Trial signup completion page (URL-based or GA4 event)
- Form submission event (if multi-step trial flow)
- Google Ads tag + GA4 linked + import of GA4 goals into Google Ads
- Confirm tracking fires correctly in Tag Assistant before any campaign goes live

## Bidding Strategy Recommendations

### Strategy by Campaign

| Campaign | Current Strategy | CPC Cap | Upgrade Trigger | Target CPA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand Defense | Target IS (95%) | €2.00 | N/A | N/A |
| Competitor Keywords | Maximize Clicks | €4.00 | 30 conversions | €45–60 |
| Pain Point Keywords | Maximize Clicks | €3.00 | 30 conversions | €40–55 |
| Use Case Keywords | Maximize Clicks | €3.50 | 30 conversions | €35–50 |

**Rationale for Max CPC caps:**
The not-a-bot.ai product is priced at €19/month. At a conservative 6-month average customer lifetime, LTV is approximately €114. A CPL of €45–60 represents a CAC:LTV ratio of roughly 1:2, which is acceptable for a SaaS product with low churn risk and low support overhead. Caps are set to prevent runaway spend before smart bidding has sufficient conversion data.

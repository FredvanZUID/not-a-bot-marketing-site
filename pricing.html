Questions about pricing

After the 5-day trial, you choose a plan and enter your payment details to continue. If you don't upgrade, your agent is automatically paused — no charges applied. Your data stays safe for 30 days in case you want to reactivate.

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time via your dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply from the next billing cycle. When upgrading, the price difference is prorated for the remaining days in your billing period.

A token is roughly 4 characters of text. Each conversation with your AI agent uses tokens for both the user's message and the agent's response. A typical customer question and answer exchanges about 200–400 tokens. On the Basic plan at 100,000 tokens, this covers approximately 250–500 conversations per month.

You pay per agent per month — not per conversation or per user. Each agent is a dedicated AI trained on your specific documents. You can create multiple agents for different purposes (e.g., one for customer service, one for bookings) and each agent has its own subscription.

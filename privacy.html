1. Who we are

not-a-bot.ai is a software-as-a-service platform for deploying AI agents trained on your business data. The platform is operated by not-a-bot.ai BV, a company registered in Belgium.

For all privacy-related matters, you can reach our Data Protection contact at: privacy@not-a-bot.ai.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide and improve our service:

  • Account data: Your name, email address, company name, and optionally your phone number when you register an account.
  • Usage data: Agent interactions, pseudonymized conversation logs, and feature usage statistics to help us improve the platform.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies required for the secure operation of the platform.
  • Payment data: Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We never store or have access to your card details.
  • Lead data: Inputs submitted through our ROI Calculator (business type, question volume, average handling time) and associated contact information.

3. Why we collect this data (legal basis)

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under GDPR Article 6:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): To create your account, deliver the platform, and fulfill the subscription you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): To improve our service, detect abuse and security threats, and send relevant product updates to existing customers.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For marketing emails — we only send these when you have given explicit opt-in consent, such as via the checkbox on our ROI Calculator form.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): To comply with invoicing, tax, and accounting requirements under applicable law.

4. How long we retain your data

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account, plus a 30-day recovery window.
  • Conversation logs: 90-day rolling retention by default. Enterprise plan customers can configure this window in their account settings.
  • Lead and form data: Retained for 24 months from collection, or until you unsubscribe from communications.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years to comply with Belgian and EU accounting law.

5. Who we share your data with

We use a minimal set of trusted sub-processors, all operating under data processing agreements that require them to protect your data:

  • Supabase — EU-based database hosting and storage
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Netlify — Website and application hosting
  • Resend — Transactional and marketing email delivery

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties. We do not use your data for advertising on external platforms.

6. Your rights under GDPR

As a data subject under the GDPR, you have the following rights. You can exercise any of these by contacting us at privacy@not-a-bot.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

  • Right of access (Art. 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)): Withdraw previously given consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise your rights, email privacy@not-a-bot.ai. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

7. Cookies

We use cookies sparingly and only where necessary:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Session cookies and CSRF protection tokens required for secure platform operation. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference cookies: To remember your language selection between visits.
  • Analytics: We use privacy-first, cookieless analytics. No third-party analytics cookies are set on this website.
  • Advertising/tracking cookies: None. We do not use advertising networks or cross-site tracking.

By using not-a-bot.ai, you consent to strictly necessary cookies. For all other cookies, we ask for your explicit consent before setting them.

8. Data transfers

All personal data is processed and stored within the European Union. We do not transfer personal data to third countries outside the EU/EEA without ensuring adequate protections are in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46 GDPR).

9. Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest for all database storage
  • Role-based access controls — staff access only what they need
  • Regular security reviews and dependency audits
  • Infrastructure hosted on EU-based servers with SOC 2-compliant providers

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and affected users without undue delay.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will notify registered users by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect. The date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent revision.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the platform after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

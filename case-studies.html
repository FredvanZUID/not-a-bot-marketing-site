Vacation Rentals

Sunstay

47 properties · Côte d'Azur + Belgium

Sunstay manages 47 vacation properties across the Côte d'Azur and the Belgian coast. By the time they came to not-a-bot.ai, their operations team was spending 80 hours a month answering the same 12 questions: check-in codes, wifi passwords, house rules, parking, nearest supermarket. Different guests, identical questions, every single week.

They tried two competitor platforms before not-a-bot.ai. One gave guests confidently wrong information about amenities. The other required an external consultant to set up and still couldn't handle French and Dutch in the same conversation. Both were abandoned within a month.

Setup with not-a-bot.ai took 31 minutes. The operations manager uploaded one guest guide per property cluster, tested it for an afternoon, and activated it. By the end of week one, 68% of guest messages were answered without human intervention. By month three, that number was 72%. The team now focuses on hospitality: pre-arrival welcome calls, property improvements, relationship management with local services. The inbox is no longer where their day begins and ends.

Professional Services

Studio Merckx

4-person architecture firm · Brussels

Studio Merckx is a four-person architecture firm in Brussels. Every week, Thomas and his team were spending three hours on intake questions from prospective clients — pricing, timelines, what the design process looks like, what to expect on the first site visit. Not because the questions were complex, but because every new prospect needed the same explanation.

The firm uploaded their service brochure, a detailed FAQ document, and their project timeline guide. The agent went live in 24 minutes. It now handles the first layer of prospect qualification: it explains the Studio's process, shares typical project timelines by category, answers pricing range questions, and offers to book a discovery call.

The result is not fewer client interactions — it's better ones. By the time a prospect speaks to Thomas, they've already read the FAQ, they understand the fee structure, and they're arriving with specific, substantive questions. Meetings are shorter. The conversion rate on discovery calls improved because unqualified leads self-select out before the call ever happens.

HR & Recruitment

Talent One

Recruitment agency · Ghent · 200+ active job listings

Talent One runs a three-recruiter team in Ghent managing over 200 active job listings at any given time. Before not-a-bot.ai, 40% of recruiter time was going to first-line candidate questions — same questions, different candidates, every day. What is the remote policy for this role? Is the salary negotiable? What does the interview process look like? How long until you hear back?

They built a candidate FAQ agent trained on their job listing format, their standard process documentation, and a set of role-specific Q&A documents for their ten most active client verticals. Setup took 35 minutes including testing across three dummy candidate conversations.

Within the first month, 60% of incoming candidate messages were resolved without recruiter involvement. Recruiters now use their time for what they're actually good at: reading between the lines on a CV, running structured competency interviews, building relationships with hiring managers. Candidates get answers on Sunday morning. The team works Monday to Friday, on real talent assessment — not inbox management.
